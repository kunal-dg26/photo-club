# Shutterbugs Photography Club

Shutterbugs is the photography club for students on our campus. We go
on photo walks, share tips on light, framing and editing, and hold a
small exhibition every year. A phone camera is all you need to join.

## The club page

Open `index.html` in any browser to see our one-page website.

## Meeting days

- Wednesday, 5 pm, in the seminar hall
- Saturday, 7 am, photo walk from the main gate

## Learn the basics

New to photography? Start with the
[rule of thirds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rule_of_thirds).
